About TechLoop

Built for people who love tech

A daily digest keeping curious minds across Ireland, the UK and Europe connected to the world of technology.

TechLoop was built for one simple reason: keeping up with tech news shouldn't feel like a second job.

We're a daily tech digest designed for curious minds across Ireland, the UK, and Europe — people who care about what's happening in AI, gadgets, startups, gaming, and innovation, but don't have hours to spend scrolling through dozens of websites.

Every day, TechLoop automatically scans the best tech sources on the web, uses AI to cut through the noise, and delivers the stories that actually matter — clear, concise, and straight to the point.
