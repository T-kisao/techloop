26 May 2026 · 15:01 UTC

In a move to tackle the rising issue of AI-generated music, Universal Music Group has successfully renewed its agreement with TikTok to combat unauthorized music usage on the platform. This development comes on the same day that Qashio, a fintech company, announced the official opening of its European headquarters, aiming to create 50 new jobs in the region. In the world of gaming, the June PlayStation Plus lineup has been unveiled, with Xbox's popular survival game Grounded making its way to the platform. Separately, Apple TV is set to receive a raft of new features in June, while Spotify now offers users the option to stream narrated magazine articles alongside its music and podcast offerings. Additionally, the gaming world is reeling from several big announcements, including the completion of development on Destiny 2 by Bungie.
